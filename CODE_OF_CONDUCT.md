# Code of Conduct

Sonic Lab follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, welcome people of every background
and level of experience, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment, insults, personal or
political attacks and publishing others' private information are not tolerated – in issues, pull
requests, discussions and any other project space.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently exclude participants
who do not follow this code.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately via the e-mail address on the GitHub
profile of [@krocon](https://github.com/krocon). Reports are handled confidentially.
